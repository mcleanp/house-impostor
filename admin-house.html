House Impostor — house editor

Edits the shared house map (floors · rooms · doors · levels · chores) and writes house.json on the server. This is the same map the in-game Setup screen edits — here you get the whole thing on one page, no game needed. Rooms are zones; a door is an edge between two rooms; a chore belongs to a room. A room with no doors is unreachable.

Same key as the admin page (house unless ADMIN_KEY was changed).

Floors

Floors are the levels of the house. Rooms sit on a floor; levels pick which floors are in play.

Rooms

The id is what the ESP32 advertises (HB-<id>) — change it and you must reflash that board. The name is what players see.

Doors

↔

You can only walk through a door. Hallways are just rooms with doors to everything (the landings).

Levels

A level is a set of floors the host can pick at Start. Each player gets 4 chores drawn from the rooms on the chosen level.
